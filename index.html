Why it matters

When the package bends, reliability breaks

Stacking silicon, interposers, and memory bonds materials with very different expansion rates. As the assembly heats and cools, those mismatches make the whole package bow and twist — and that warpage is a first-order reliability risk in modern 2.5D and 3D integration.

🌡️

Heat makes it move

Every material in the stack expands by a different amount. Cycle the package from assembly to operating temperature and those mismatches build internal stress that warps the whole structure.

⚠️

Warpage causes failures

Too much bow leads to cracked dies, delaminated layers, and open solder joints — and it makes assembly yield fall. Catching it early is far cheaper than finding it on the line.

🔁

It has to be checked often

Floorplanning, stack-up choices, and material selection all change how a package warps. Designers need a warpage answer on every iteration — not once, at the very end.

Input & output

One description in, a warpage map you can turn over

Bring the stack straight from the ecosystem: WarpStack reads the design, builds the mechanical model itself, and every method writes the same result format — so the view, the comparison and the downstream script are identical whichever one answered.

INPUT · STANDARDS

🧩 IEEE 3Dblox & native JSON

Import designs described in IEEE 3Dblox (.3dbx) — the IEEE-standard language for physical stacking, dimensions, and connectivity in 2.5D/3D-IC — or describe the stack directly in WarpStack's floorplan JSON. Recursive includes, rotations, mirrors and molding autofill are handled on the way in: a production-class accelerator package imports to 457 mechanical layers, 273 footprint regions and 138 instances, each layer resolved through its full thickness.

INPUT · LAYOUT

🔌 ODB++ & IPC-2581 layouts

Point WarpStack at a substrate or board layout in ODB++ or IPC-2581. It reads the copper and drills of every layer itself and builds a model whose stiffness and expansion follow the copper across the board, so no hand-made copper-density maps. Dies and parts sit on the layout as simple bodies or as full 3Dblox models.

INPUT · MATERIALS

📐 Properties from a profile

Modulus, Poisson ratio and CTE come from a material profile you supply or the bundled library — never inferred. An unmatched material stops the run rather than quietly guessing, which is what makes a result reproducible.

OUTPUT · INTERACTIVE

🗺️ Turn it over, slice it

Rotate the 3D stack and warpage surface, cut XZ/YZ cross-sections on a slider, and exaggerate the z-scale to see a few microns of bow on a 60 mm package — in a self-contained HTML report you can send on.

OUTPUT · COMPARE

⚖️ Signed difference maps

Put any two answers side by side — two methods, two resolutions, two material sets — and read the signed difference across the whole surface, not just the peak-to-peak number.

OUTPUT · SCRIPTABLE

📤 Report and raw data

Warpage CSVs, geometry.json and simulation_report.json out of every run — ready to feed straight into your own flow.

Workflow · agentic integration

Built for agentic EDA flows

WarpStack is designed to drop straight into agentic EDA and system-design workflows. A first-class CLI and structured data interface let autonomous design agents call a full 3D solve, WarpStack-MOR, or WarpStack-GNN, read back machine-readable warpage maps and peak-to-peak margins, and feed them into floorplanning, stack-up, and material-selection loops. With millisecond AI inference, warpage finally runs at the speed of the loop itself.

  • Agentic-flow ready — built to be driven by autonomous EDA and system-design agents, and to plug into any agentic flow.
  • CLI-first — every warpage analysis is scriptable from the command line; nothing in the loop needs a GUI.
  • Structured data interface — machine-readable floorplans in; warpage surfaces, peak-to-peak bow, and signed difference maps out.
  • Warpage-aware optimization — at 19.4 ms per evaluation, WarpStack-GNN lets agents sweep thousands of material and thermal design points in a live loop.
  • Reliability sign-off — screen the whole space with WarpStack-MOR or WarpStack-GNN; sign off the critical designs with a full 3D solve before tape-out.
